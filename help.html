Birdmap setup

Add the app to your home screen, then set up the two Safari bookmarks that refresh the BirdGuides layer and your year list from your phone.

1. Add to home screen

Open the app in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen from then on, and eBird data loads live every time you open it.

2. BirdGuides bookmarklet

eBird is live, but BirdGuides needs your logged-in session, so the app imports it from a BirdGuides page you have open. Set this up once:

aDrag this link to your bookmarks bar on a Mac, or on the iPhone: bookmark this page, then edit the bookmark and replace its address with the code in the box below. Name it bgx.

Import BirdGuides → Birdmap

bIn Safari, open BirdGuides sightings, filter to the county and the last 7 days, set results to 1000, and Search.
cTap the bgx bookmark. You'll land back in Birdmap with the new reports merged in. Sightings at sites the app doesn't have coordinates for are skipped and listed in the message.

3. Year list bookmarklet

Open your eBird year list in Safari and tap this bookmark; it sends the species to the app so "Still to see" stays current as you add birds.

Update year list → Birdmap

Notes

Imports are stored on the phone only. The region, filters and skipped sites are remembered per device. If something looks stale, tap Refresh in the header; eBird data refreshes automatically after 15 minutes in the background.
